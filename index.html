About This Project

This interactive platform provides 3D visualizations of molecular and crystal structures to enhance chemistry education. Using cutting-edge web technologies like Three.js, these visualizations allow students to:

  • ✓ Rotate, zoom, and inspect atomic structures from all angles
  • ✓ Understand bond types and molecular geometry
  • ✓ Visualize electron behavior and delocalization
  • ✓ Learn through interactive, immersive exploration

How to Use

🖱️ Rotate

Left click and drag to rotate the structure in 3D space.

⚙️ Zoom

Scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in and out of the structure.

🖱️ Pan

Right click and drag to pan and reposition the view.

⏸️ Control

Use the pause/resume button to toggle automatic rotation.
